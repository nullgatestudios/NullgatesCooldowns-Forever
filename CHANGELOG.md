# Changelog

All notable changes to Nullgate's Cooldowns - Forever.

## [1.0.0-beta.1] - 2026-11-20

First test version, looking for groups to try it.

### New

- **Group cooldown list**: Rebirth, Soulstone, Reincarnation, Divine Intervention, Innervate, Mana Tide, Lay on Hands, Blessing of Protection, Shield Wall, Last Stand, Fear Ward, Power Infusion, Challenging Shout, Challenging Roar and Tranquilizing Shot for everyone in your party or raid, grouped by type: ready, minutes left, or unknown.
- **Built alongside the game's Cooldown Manager**: your own cooldowns stay there; this addon adds everyone else's, including the ones the Cooldown Manager doesn't list (Reincarnation, Soulstone and Tranquilizing Shot).
- **Forever cooldown times** read from the client data (Lay on Hands is 20 minutes and Shield Wall 15 there).
- **Exact timers from players running the addon**, including talent reductions; for everyone else, timers start from the casts seen. Talent cooldowns appear once a player has them.
- **Unknown is never ready**: players out of range, offline or dead with no known timer show as unknown.
- **Click to ask**: click a ready cooldown to whisper the player, for example "Rebirth on Bob, please".
- **Optional chat line for you** when someone uses Rebirth or a Soulstone.
- **Settings** in its own window or inside Nullgate's Addon Config, and **`/ncd check`** for bug reports.
- Available in English, Deutsch, Español, Français, Italiano, Português (Brasil), Русский, 한국어, 简体中文 and 繁體中文.
